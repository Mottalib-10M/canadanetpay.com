Privacy policy of this Canadian salary calculator 2026

How Canada Net Pay handles your data. No personal salary or financial data is collected, stored, or transmitted. All calculations run in your browser.

By Radif Partners, passionate about personal finance, Éditeur de calculateurs et de guides pratiques

Last updated: January 15, 2025

Summary

Canada Net Pay is a static website. All calculations run entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit any salary or personal information.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. Specifically:

  • No salary, income, or financial information is sent to any server.
  • No account registration is required.
  • No cookies are used for tracking purposes.
  • No personal information is sold to third parties.

How the Calculator Works

All tax calculations are performed client-side in your web browser using JavaScript. The numbers you enter never leave your device. When you share a URL with calculator parameters (e.g., salary amount and province), that information is encoded in the URL hash fragment, which is not sent to servers.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics (such as aggregate page view counts) that do not track individual users or use cookies. No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics.

Local Storage

The website may use your browser's local storage to save your theme preference (light/dark mode). This data stays on your device and is not transmitted anywhere.

Third-Party Services

The website is hosted on static hosting infrastructure. We do not use third-party advertising, tracking pixels, or social media widgets that could collect your data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us.

Official sources

Every rate on this page comes from the publications below. No figure is taken from a third-party summary.

  • Canada Revenue Agency, income tax rates

    Federal and provincial brackets, indexed each January, for the current and previous years.

  • Canada Revenue Agency, CPP contributions

    Contribution rates, the basic exemption and both earnings ceilings, including CPP2.

  • Employment Insurance premium rates

    Employee and employer premium rates and maximum insurable earnings, set annually.

  • Revenu Québec, income tax rates

    Quebec administers its own income tax, its pension plan and its parental insurance plan.
