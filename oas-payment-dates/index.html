OAS Payment Dates 2026 and amounts

The twelve days on which Old Age Security, the Guaranteed Income Supplement and CPP are paid in 2026, with the amount of each benefit this quarter.

Old Age Security is paid once a month, near the end of the month, on the same days as the Canada Pension Plan. In 2026 the twelve payment dates run from the 25th to the 29th, except in December, when the payment is brought forward to the 22nd so that it arrives before the holidays. Each payment covers the month in which it is made. The Guaranteed Income Supplement, the Allowance and the Allowance for the Survivor follow the same calendar. Amounts change four times a year, in January, April, July and October, when they are adjusted to the Consumer Price Index, and in July the supplement and the recovery tax deduction are also recalculated on the previous year's income. This page gives the official 2026 calendar, the maximum amount of each benefit for the current quarter, and what to do when a payment does not arrive on the expected date.

How the payment dates are set

OAS is paid monthly, for the current month, in the last days of that month rather than on a fixed date. The day moves to avoid weekends and statutory holidays, which is why it ranges from the 25th to the 29th in 2026. December is the exception: the payment is brought forward to just before Christmas, on Tuesday, December 22 this year. Because CPP and OAS share the calendar, a pensioner who receives both sees two deposits on the same day, one from each program.

Your first payment arrives in the month after your application is approved, or the month after your 65th birthday if you were enrolled automatically, and covers that month. Back payments, when an application is late, are limited to the eleven months before the month Service Canada received it, on top of the current month.

What changes from one payment to the next

  • Indexation, four times a year. The amounts change with the payments of January, April, July and October. The pension cannot fall, even when prices do.
  • The 75th birthday. The 10% increase starts with the payment for the month after the birthday.
  • July. The new payment period starts: GIS and the Allowance are recalculated on the previous year's income, and the recovery tax deduction is reset on the latest assessed return.
  • Tax withheld. Voluntary federal tax deductions, if you asked for them, stay the same until you change them.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the next OAS payment in 2026?
The remaining Old Age Security payments of 2026 arrive on Wednesday, October 28, Thursday, November 26 and Tuesday, December 22. CPP is paid on the same days. Each payment is for the month in which it arrives, and the amount is the one in force for October to December 2026. With direct deposit, the money is in the account on the payment date itself.
Why is the December OAS payment early?
Payments normally fall in the last three banking days of the month, but December is brought forward to Tuesday, December 22 so that the money arrives before the holidays. It is the December payment, not an advance on January: the January 2027 payment will still arrive at the end of January, so the gap between the two is longer than usual and worth planning for.
How much is the OAS payment this month?
For October to December 2026, a full OAS pension is $762.50 a month from 65 to 74 and $838.75 from 75. A partial pension is the fraction of years in Canada over 40. The amount actually deposited can be lower if income tax or recovery tax is withheld, and higher if the Guaranteed Income Supplement is paid, since it arrives in the same deposit.
What should I do if my OAS payment has not arrived?
Wait three working days after the scheduled date: a deposit can be delayed by the bank, and a cheque by the post. After that, contact Service Canada, which can trace or reissue the payment. Check first in My Service Canada Account that your banking details are current; a closed account is the most common reason a deposit fails.
Do OAS and GIS arrive on the same day?
Yes. The OAS pension, the Guaranteed Income Supplement, the Allowance and the Allowance for the Survivor are all paid on the same dates as the Canada Pension Plan. The amounts of OAS and GIS are shown separately on the payment details in My Service Canada Account and on the T4A(OAS) slip at year end.
When does the OAS amount change?
In January, April, July and October, when the pension is adjusted to the Consumer Price Index; it never goes down. The October 2026 adjustment was 1.4%. The recovery tax deduction changes in July, when the income of the previous return takes over: from July 2026 it is based on 2025 income and the $93,454 threshold.
